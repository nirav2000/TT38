Why learning up to 20× times tables helps with fractions

When you know multiplication facts up to 20×, fraction work becomes faster and less stressful because fractions depend on multiplication patterns.

1) Faster common denominators

To add 3/8 + 5/12 you need the least common denominator. Knowing 8 and 12 facts quickly reveals 24. That speed comes from knowing factors and multiples beyond just 12×.

2) Better simplification

For 42/56, spotting that both divide by 14 (or 7) is easier when 14× and related facts are familiar.

3) Scaling fractions mentally

Equivalent fractions like 7/15 = 14/30 = 21/45 are immediate when you can multiply confidently through 20×.

4) Real-life confidence

  • Recipes: scaling ingredients by 3/2 or 5/4
  • Percentages: 15%, 20%, 12.5%
  • Ratios: maps, speed, unit rates

Tip: practice times tables to 20× and then practice fraction conversions with timed rounds for best transfer.
